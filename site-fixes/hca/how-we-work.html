How we work

Agency know-how. Delivered like software.

A conversation first. A clear scope before any build. We use the first hour to understand your agency, then decide together whether a strategy, a build, some coaching, or no project at all is the right next step.

Four stops

Four stops. Not every agency needs all four.

Every stop ends with a decision, and stopping is a perfectly good outcome.

Discuss

Your ambitions

We walk through how your agency wins business today, where the friction sits, and what good looks like. Where you want to get to, what's realistic and what's not.

You leave with a view of whether there's something worth exploring.

◆ Decision: explore further, or stop here.

Map

Strategy

We look at your process, positioning, pipeline, tools and people, and compare the options: fix the process, use a tool you have, build something tailored, or leave it alone.

You leave with an honest map of where AI helps your new business, with a straight recommendation.

◆ Decision: build, buy, fix the process or leave it.

Build

AI-integrated

If a build is worth doing, we agree its purpose, scope, tools, access, review points and who owns each decision. Price and timing are agreed before build work starts.

You leave with a working solution, fitted to your tools, with documentation.

◆ Decision: accept the handover, or adjust.

Coach

People & evolve

Optional, agreed separately. We coach your team, run the grown-up conversations alongside them, and refine the tools as the agency grows.

You leave with a team that uses it, and refinements as you grow.

◆ Decision: continue, pause or end.

In writing

Agreed in writing, before any build.

Tools & accessWhich systems, which permissions and which data, checked first.
Review pointsWho owns each decision, and where a person checks the work.
Testing & handoverHow it's tested and handed over, and how you'll judge it useful.
