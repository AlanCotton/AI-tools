What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, agency, email address, phone number (optional) and your message.
  • Email and calls: what you send us when you email hello@hca.uk.com.
  • Booking a call or demo: when you choose a time, you use Google Calendar's booking page, which collects your name, email address and any notes you add.
  • Website logs: our hosting provider keeps basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and secure the site.

How we use it, and our lawful basis

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, arrange and hold the call you asked for, and, if we work together, to set up and run that work. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries and, where relevant, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We don't sell your data and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.
