Hummingbird is led by Jane Heath, a successful entrepreneur with over 30 years’ experience founding and scaling businesses. She founded HCA – one of the UK’s first new-business agencies – and is widely credited with creating an entirely new sector, not just a business.

That recognition led the UK government to commission her to author a paper on the future of small business for The Parliamentary Review. A business mentor and go-to-market strategist, she has a proven track record of driving revenue, profit and performance.
