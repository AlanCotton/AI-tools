Vive LibreLive free · Costa Blanca

Definitely not an estate agent – we're your independent partner from first daydream to first morning coffee on the terrace.

Vive Libre is a property and relocation service, for people making the Mediterranean their home. Buying abroad should be one of the great adventures of your life – too often it becomes a maze of agents, paperwork and quiet anxieties. We take that weight off your hands, finding the right area, the right home and the right people around you, so all that's left is the part you came for.

We're one partner for the move, the home, and the life around it. The whole journey, not the transaction – banks, lawyers and the gestor; builders, interiors and maintenance; health cover, driving licences, schools and settling-in support. We set up your whole life here, not just the keys to a house.

And because we've made the move ourselves, we help you avoid the pitfalls: why renders mislead, why south-facing matters in winter, how the agent system really works. Covering the Costa Blanca North from Villajoyosa up to Dénia – including Altea, Moraira and Jávea – we guide you from a first, no-pressure conversation through to the day you settle in.
