Why us

Learning that’s built around your business – not theory.

We turn the real conversations your team has every day into confidence, skill, and measurable commercial results.

Most training is generic, theory-led, and forgotten by the following week. Ours is the opposite: pragmatic, interactive, and built entirely around your business, your people, and the challenges they actually face every day.

Ahead of every programme we spend dedicated one-to-one time with each participant to understand their individual challenges, confidence gaps, and performance blockers. That means the session is focused on the things that genuinely move the needle – not a checklist of slides.

The format is deliberately non-classroom: a high-energy mix of trainer-led input, peer-to-peer knowledge sharing, real-world analysis, exercises, games and immediate feedback. A closing ‘what we learnt today’ session reinforces the key takeaways so new skills actually stick.

And to make sure everyone gets the time and attention they need, we cap every session at six participants – so the learning stays personal, honest, and commercially focused.

Client results

What changed after the workshop.

Mid-sized PR agency

Replacing lost revenue, fast

“There was a change in behaviour straight after the training day. The team had the confidence to sell, and within three months we’d closed the revenue gap and grown beyond it.”

Managing Director, mid-sized PR agency

The challenge
The agency lost two large clients in quick succession. It wanted to keep its senior people, and it had only a short window to replace the business, so it needed to put as much resource into new business as it could afford.
What we did
We ran an interactive new business workshop for the whole senior management team, then mentored each member individually. The workshop format helps overcome the hesitancy that often holds back otherwise very capable people, and the mentoring helped each of them work outside their comfort zone.
The result
The MD saw an immediate change in behaviour after the training day. The team was re-energised and had the confidence to sell. Within three months the agency had closed the revenue gap and grown beyond it.
B2B travel specialist

Turning a junior team into a sales team

“The team started working together and building on each other’s strengths. We brought in more sales the following year than in the two years before.”

Sales and Marketing Director, B2B travel specialist

The challenge
Twelve junior salespeople were working with very little input or guidance from senior management.
What we did
We started with a two-day workshop covering the whole sales process, from researching the ideal client profile through to renewals. We then mentored the team for six months. As two of them grew into team leaders, we coached them in their new roles while they mentored their own teams.
The result
According to the sales and marketing director, the team started working as a team and building on each other’s strengths. In the following year they brought in more sales than in the previous two years put together.
Small tech company

Building a growth culture, and finding a new business lead

“I had no idea one of my team was such a natural salesperson. Without the workshop, that talent would have gone unnoticed.”

Managing Director, small tech company

The challenge
The company wanted its whole team of six to be more focused on growth.
What we did
We ran a hybrid workshop covering the principles of sales, client service, retention and growth. One of the account managers showed a real aptitude for business development, so we mentored them through the move into leading new business for the company.
The result
The MD hadn’t realised that one of his team was such a natural salesperson. Without the workshop, that talent might well have gone unnoticed.
