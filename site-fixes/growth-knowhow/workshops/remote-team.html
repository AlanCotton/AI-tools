Managing a Remote Team

Managing a Remote Team Workshop

Lead distributed teams with clarity, trust, and momentum.

This practical, high-impact workshop is designed to help managers lead remote and hybrid teams with confidence, structure, and genuine connection.

Built around the real challenges of managing people you don't sit beside every day, it focuses on the habits, conversations, and rhythms that keep distributed teams aligned, motivated, and performing – wherever they work from.

Purpose. To help managers build trust, maintain accountability, and create a high-performing team culture across distance, time zones, and hybrid working patterns.

What you'll learn

Participants will learn how to:

  • set clear expectations and goals that work without daily oversight
  • run focused, energising remote meetings and one-to-ones
  • communicate with clarity across chat, email, video and async tools
  • build trust and psychological safety in a distributed team
  • keep people accountable without micromanaging
  • spot disengagement, isolation, and burnout early
  • give feedback and handle difficult conversations remotely
  • create connection, belonging, and team culture at a distance
  • balance synchronous and asynchronous ways of working

Core workshop areas

  • the mindset and habits of effective remote leadership
  • setting expectations, goals, and ways of working
  • choosing the right channel for the right message
  • running effective remote meetings and one-to-ones
  • asynchronous communication and documentation
  • building trust and psychological safety across distance
  • accountability and outcome-based management
  • recognising disengagement, isolation, and burnout
  • feedback and difficult conversations over video
  • creating connection, ritual, and team culture
  • managing hybrid fairness and inclusion
  • time zones, boundaries, and sustainable working rhythms

How it's delivered

The session is delivered in a highly interactive, non-classroom format using a mix of trainer input, discussion, exercises and group learning – and can be run virtually or in person. This ensures the content feels relevant, practical, and immediately usable. A high-energy ‘what we learnt today’ session reinforces the key learnings and helps participants embed new skills.

Ahead of the session, dedicated one-to-one time is spent with each participant to understand individual challenges, confidence gaps, and performance blockers. This ensures the final workshop is highly focused, relevant, and built around the real issues affecting team and individual effectiveness.
