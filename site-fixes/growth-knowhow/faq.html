FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers about our workshops, how they work and how to book.

Who delivers the workshops?

Jane Heath and Alan Cotton design and deliver every programme personally. Between them they have spent three decades building, training and growing commercial teams, and they founded the go-to-market (GTM) agency HCA.

Who are the workshops for?

Agency and B2B teams – the people who pitch, manage clients, sell complex services and collaborate across departments every day.

How many people can attend?

A maximum of six participants per session, so everyone gets genuine attention and honest feedback.

How long is a workshop?

Each workshop runs as a one-day intensive or a two-day deep development programme. Managing a Remote Team is also available as a half-day virtual session.

Is the content tailored to our business?

Yes. Before the workshop we spend one-to-one time with each participant to understand their challenges, confidence gaps and performance blockers, then build the session around your products or services, roles and real conversations.

Can workshops be run virtually?

Managing a Remote Team can be run virtually or in person. [TO CONFIRM: whether the other workshops can be delivered virtually or as hybrid sessions]

What happens after the workshop?

A closing ‘what we learnt today’ session embeds the skills on the day. We can also stay close afterwards, with optional follow-up check-ins, ongoing mentoring and continuous-improvement sessions.

How do we get started?

Send us a message through the enquiry form, book a call in our calendar or email hello@growth-knowhow.com.
