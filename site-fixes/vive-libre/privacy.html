What we collect, why we collect it, who sees it and the rights you have under the GDPR. Last updated 16 November 2026.

The data we collect

  • When you make an enquiry: your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), your timeline, budget, areas of interest and anything you tell us in your message.
  • When you become a client: the details we need to help you buy and move, such as identity documents, your NIE, proof of funds or mortgage information, and family circumstances that affect your search (for example, schools).
  • When you email, call or WhatsApp us: the content of our conversations and correspondence. WhatsApp messages are also handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy.
  • When you join our mailing list: your email address, and whether you open or click our emails if our mailing service measures this.
  • When you visit the site: your IP address and basic technical information, which our hosting provider records to run and secure the site.

We don't ask for special category data (such as health or religion). If you choose to share it (for example, to explain accessibility needs in a home), we will only use it for that purpose.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We share it only where we need to:

  • Professionals you choose to work with: lawyers, notaries, gestors, banks, mortgage brokers, surveyors, schools and other specialists we introduce. We only pass on your details with your agreement, and each of them is responsible for how they use your data.
  • Our service providers: who process data on our behalf under contract: Netlify (website hosting and contact form) and Google Workspace (email and documents).
  • Authorities: where the law requires it.

Transfers outside Europe

Some of our service providers, including Netlify and Google, are based in the United States. Where your data leaves the EU or UK, we rely on recognised safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go further: up to 12 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client files: for as long as the law requires. Anti-money-laundering records must be kept for 10 years, and tax records for the statutory period.
  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (and the UK GDPR, if you live in the UK) you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted, where we no longer need it
  • Restrict or object to how we use it
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it

To use any of these rights, email hello@vivelibrespain.com. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, or, in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Cookies and browser storage

This site doesn't use cookies, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

  • Fonts: our typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which see your IP address. Google doesn't set cookies when it does this.

If we ever add analytics or other cookies that need consent, we'll update this page and ask for your permission first.

Keeping your data safe

The site is served over an encrypted connection, access to client information is limited to the Vive Libre team, and we only work with providers who meet European data protection standards.

Changes to this notice

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Questions about your data? Email hello@vivelibrespain.com and one of us will get back to you.
