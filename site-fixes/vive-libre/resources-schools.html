International schools on the Costa Blanca North.

Curricula, catchment, waiting lists and fees: how relocating families choose well.

Your options

  • International schools: British curriculum (GCSE and A level) or International Baccalaureate, taught mainly in English
  • Spanish state schools: free, local, and a fast route to fluency, especially for younger children
  • Concertado schools: state-subsidised, often religious, with modest fees

Choose the school, then the house

For families, the school run shapes daily life more than almost anything else. The best international schools are concentrated in a few areas, so we look at schools alongside your property search, not after it.

Plan ahead

  • Popular year groups fill up, so contact schools early and ask about waiting lists
  • Visit in term time, and talk to parents as well as staff
  • Check what fees include, as transport, lunches, uniform and exams can add up
  • Ask how the school supports children arriving with little Spanish or English

Age matters

Younger children usually adapt to a Spanish-language school quickly. Older children close to exams often do better continuing a familiar curriculum.
