Avoiding scams

Most purchases go smoothly, but international buyers can be a target. A few simple rules protect you.

  • Never pay a deposit until your own lawyer has checked the property and the seller
  • Never send money because an email says bank details have changed. Always confirm by phone with someone you know
  • Be wary of pressure to sign quickly, or requests to pay part of the price in cash
  • Check the seller is the registered owner, or has the legal right to sell
  • Use a bank or regulated currency provider, never an informal transfer

Buying off plan

New-build and off-plan homes can be excellent value, but you're paying for something that doesn't exist yet. Spanish law gives buyers strong protections, so make sure they're in place before you pay anything.

  • Stage payments before completion should be covered by a bank guarantee or insurance policy in your name
  • Check the developer's track record, and that the building licence has been granted
  • Read the full specification, not just the brochure, and have it attached to the contract
  • Ask what's planned on neighbouring plots, as renders never show the next building
  • Agree in writing what happens if the build is delayed

The legal checks we insist on

Your lawyer carries out these checks before you commit. We make sure none are skipped.

  • The nota simple from the land registry, which confirms ownership and any debts or charges against the property
  • The land registry and the catastro match: size, boundaries and buildings
  • Planning status: extensions, pools and conversions are legal and registered
  • The licence of first occupation, or its equivalent, for new and recently built homes
  • No unpaid community fees, IBI or utility bills, which can follow the property
  • For rural homes, that the land and buildings are legal and the plot is what you think it is

How the buying process works

  • Reservation: a small deposit takes the home off the market while your lawyer runs the checks
  • Private purchase contract: often an arras contract, with a deposit (commonly around 10%) and clear terms if either side pulls out
  • Completion: you sign the deed at the notary, pay the balance and receive the keys
  • Registration: your lawyer registers the deed in your name at the land registry

The things summer buyers overlook

  • Winter sun: in winter the sun sits low, and a north-facing home or a plot in a valley can lose direct sun for months
  • Multiple listings: the same home is often listed by several agencies at different prices; we search across them all, and off-market
  • Renders and photos: they flatter, so we check the view, the light and what's around the property in person

A survey is worth it

Spanish purchases don't routinely include a survey, so many buyers skip one. For older villas especially, an independent survey can reveal damp, structural problems or costly repairs before you commit.

Good questions

What a buyer's agent actually does.

What does a buyer's agent do?

We work for you, the buyer. We source and secure the right home, save you time and money, negotiate hard on your behalf, and oversee the whole transaction through to completion. On the Costa Blanca we also handle the relocation around it.

How is this different from an estate agent?

An estate agent's job is to sell their own stock, so their focus is the sale. Ours is you: finding the right home and negotiating the best deal on it. We search the entire market (every agency, plus off-market) rather than steering you towards one portfolio.

How does it save me money?

Through expert, dispassionate negotiation; by spotting overpriced or problem properties before you commit; and by avoiding the expensive errors that catch out overseas buyers, from the wrong aspect to paperwork that derails a purchase.

What sort of clients do you work with?

International buyers making a considered move to the Costa Blanca North (relocating families, retirees, holiday-home owners and remote professionals) who value their time and want a trusted partner to handle the detail for them.

What's the process to work with you?

It starts with a free discovery call. If we're a good fit, we agree a brief and a fixed fee, then begin the search. From there we guide you through viewings, negotiation, purchase and settling in, with one point of contact throughout.

What can you do that I can't do myself?

You absolutely could do it yourself, with months of research, a lot of flights, and the risk of learning the local rules the expensive way. We bring the relationships, the market knowledge and the negotiating distance, and we compress the whole thing into a calm, guided experience.
