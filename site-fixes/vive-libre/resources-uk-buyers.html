Buying property in Spain as a UK citizen.

What changed after Brexit, how long you can stay, and the steps that matter most for buyers from the UK.

[TO CONFIRM: full guide content – UK-specific points such as driving licence exchange, healthcare (S1), pensions and UK tax residency]

How long you can stay

Since Brexit, UK citizens can spend up to 90 days in any rolling 180-day period across the Schengen area without a visa. Owning a home in Spain doesn't change that. To stay longer you'll need a visa, such as the non-lucrative visa or the digital nomad visa. See our visas guide.

Paperwork to start early

You'll need an NIE number and a Spanish bank account before you can buy. Apply early, as appointments can take weeks. See moving & relocation.

Costs and currency

Budget roughly 11% to 14% on top of the price for taxes and fees. If your money is in pounds, the exchange rate can change the real cost of your home by thousands, so talk to a specialist currency provider early. See the buying process and tax & money.

Wills and tax

Spanish inheritance and tax rules differ from the UK's. Take advice from a cross-border adviser who understands both countries before you buy.
