Resources · Cost of living

What you really need to live well here.

The real running costs of a home on the Costa Blanca, beyond the property price.

Running the home

  • IBI property tax and local rubbish collection charges
  • Community fees if you're in an urbanisation or apartment block
  • Electricity, water and gas: summer air conditioning and winter heating both count
  • Home insurance, plus pool and garden maintenance

Everyday life

Many people find day-to-day life more affordable than in northern Europe. Fresh food from the market, eating out and public transport are all good value. Imported brands and international schools are the exceptions.

Getting around

Most of the Costa Blanca North rewards having a car, especially in the hills. Budget for insurance, the annual ITV inspection and fuel.

Healthcare

If you aren't covered by the state system, private health insurance is widely used and good value. Premiums rise with age, so get quotes early.

Build in a buffer

Older villas, large plots and pools cost more to keep. When we shortlist homes we flag the ones that will be expensive to run, so the budget works for years, not just on completion day.
