Privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy sets out how HCA collects, uses and looks after personal data: for visitors to hca.uk.com, contacts at the agencies we work with or talk to, and anyone who gets in touch with us.

Who we are

HCA is a trading name of Chameleon Brands Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 12945712. Our registered office is Dragon House, Princes Way, Bridgend Industrial Estate, Bridgend, CF31 3AQ.

Chameleon Brands Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office under registration number ZC276789. Chameleon Brands Ltd also trades under other brand names, and each publishes its own privacy information.

Questions about this policy can go to hello@hca.uk.com, or by post to the Director at our registered office.

The personal data we collect and why

When you contact us or book a call

When you use the contact form on hca.uk.com, email us or book a call, we collect what you give us: normally your name, business, email address, phone number and message. We use it to reply to you and to arrange and prepare for a call. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests, in responding to enquiries, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

The form is handled by our website host, Netlify. After you send it you may be taken to Google Calendar to choose a time; Google then processes the details you enter there.

Clients

If you become a client, we hold your name, job title, organisation, contact details, our correspondence, meeting notes, project records and billing details, so that we can deliver our services, invoice and keep accounts. Our lawful bases are contract, legitimate interests and, for tax records, legal obligation.

Website visitors

We do not use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising or social media pixels on hca.uk.com. If we add analytics in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy. Our hosting provider keeps server logs, including IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests, in keeping the site secure and available.

Business development

We contact agencies we believe could benefit from our services, by email, phone and LinkedIn, using business contact details from public sources and professional networks. We contact people only in their professional capacity. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests, and consent where the law requires it. You can opt out at any time and we will not contact you again.

Who we share personal data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only when we need to: with service providers who process it for us under contract, our professional advisers (such as accountants, insurers and lawyers), a buyer or successor if the business is sold or restructured, and the police, regulators or others where the law requires it.

Sending personal data outside the UK

Some of our team work from the European Economic Area, which the UK recognises as providing adequate protection. Some service providers are based in the United States. Where personal data goes to them, we rely on the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How we protect personal data

Access to our systems is limited to the people who need it, and accounts are protected by multi-factor authentication. If a personal data breach affects your information and is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you, and where the law requires it we will report it to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Your rights

You have the right to get a copy of the personal data we hold about you; have it corrected; have it deleted where we no longer have a good reason to keep it; restrict or object to how we use it; object to direct marketing at any time; receive data you gave us in a portable format, where that right applies; and withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email hello@hca.uk.com. We will reply within one month, and there is no charge. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We do not make decisions about you by purely automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us first so that we can try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We review this policy at least once a year. If we make a material change, we will publish the new version here with its version number and effective date.
