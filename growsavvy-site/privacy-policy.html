Privacy policy

How we collect, use and look after your personal data.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Grow Savvy is a trading name of Chameleon Brands Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. Chameleon Brands Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. References to “we”, “us” and “our” mean Chameleon Brands Ltd trading as Grow Savvy.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, email us at hello@grow-savvy.com.

What we collect

We only collect the personal data we need to respond to you and deliver our services:

  • Enquiry details: your name, phone number, email address and the message you send through our contact form.
  • Correspondence: anything you send us by email, and notes from calls and meetings with us.
  • Client information: if you work with us, the contact details of you and your team, and the business information, plans and challenges you share with us so we can advise you.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic browser information, which our website host processes automatically to serve the site.

How we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and arrange a conversation
  • to prepare proposals and deliver our business management, AI and go-to-market services
  • to provide the mentoring, check-ins and ongoing support you have asked for
  • to manage our business relationship with you, including invoicing
  • to keep our website secure and working properly

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

Our lawful basis

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests: responding to business enquiries, delivering our services and running our business.
  • Contract: where we need your data to carry out an agreement with you or your organisation.
  • Legal obligation: keeping financial records as required by law.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers who process data on our behalf, under contracts that require them to keep it secure:

  • Vercel: hosts this website and receives contact form submissions.
  • Resend: delivers contact form enquiries to our inbox by email.
  • Google: provides our email and document tools.

Anything you share with us about your business is treated in confidence. We work with no more than five non-competing businesses at a time, and we never share one client's information with another.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of our service providers store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where data leaves the UK, we rely on adequacy regulations or appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to two years after our last contact if no work goes ahead. Client records are kept for the length of our relationship and up to six years afterwards, so we can meet our legal and accounting obligations. After that, data is securely deleted.

Cookies

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site. If we add cookies in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • ask us to delete your data
  • object to or ask us to restrict how we use your data
  • ask us to transfer your data to another organisation

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@grow-savvy.com. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first, so please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
