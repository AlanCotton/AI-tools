Grow Savvy website - Netlify upload

1. Go to app.netlify.com > Add new site > Deploy manually, and drag this zip
   (or the unzipped folder) onto the page.
2. Contact form: in the site's Forms settings, enable form detection, then
   redeploy. Under Forms > contact > Form notifications, add an email
   notification to hello@grow-savvy.com so each enquiry is emailed to you.
3. Domain: Domain management > Add a domain > grow-savvy.com.
